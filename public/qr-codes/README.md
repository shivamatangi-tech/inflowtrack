# QR Code Photos Folder (`/public/qr-codes/`)

This folder stores QR code photos and payment images used on the back of cards in inflotrack.

## How to Add Your QR Code Photos:
1. **Upload via the App**:
   - In **Cards & Goals**, flip the card to the back face.
   - Tap **"Upload QR Photo"** (or click **Edit Card**).
   - Choose any photo from your phone or computer. The app will automatically save it here and display it on the back of your card!

2. **Or Drop Files Directly Here**:
   - You can place any `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.svg`, or `.webp` file in this folder (e.g. `gpay_qr.png`, `phonepe_qr.jpg`, `paytm_qr.png`).
   - Open inflotrack, tap **"Select from QR Photo Folder"**, and select your photo with one click!

## Features:
- Rendered in **Big Size** on the back of the card.
- **Name and CVV are hidden** on the back of the card for privacy and a clean, scannable QR display.
- One-click zoom for fast scanning at merchant counters.
